# Final Verification and Quality-Gate Protocol

Run this protocol after the lead analysis is complete and before delivering any flagship, growth-utility, research-candidate, or no-go verdict.

## Mandatory Independent Reviewers

Use **two independent specialist reviewers**. When delegation is available, dispatch two separate reviewers with distinct briefs and require them to inspect the lead analysis independently. Do not provide Reviewer B with Reviewer A’s conclusions before it completes its review.

When delegation is unavailable, perform two separately scoped review passes and state in the final report that they were independent **review passes**, not separate agents. Do not claim multi-agent verification when only one analytical pass occurred.

| Reviewer | Mission | Must verify | Must reject/escalate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reviewer A: Evidence Auditor** | Audit factual integrity and source adequacy. | Product baseline, competitor names and workflows, pricing/limits, demand/traffic metrics, complaints, technical feasibility claims, and every “missing/paid/unique/first/free/unlimited” statement. | Unsupported factual statement, stale or non-primary evidence for material pricing/feature claim, “not found” posed as absence, uncited score rationale, or conflict between live product and documentation. |
| **Reviewer B: Adversarial Strategy Reviewer** | Challenge the decision logic and strategic usefulness. | Job-to-be-done, candidate completeness, buyer-choice proof, workflow advantage, quality/failure design, copy path, score consistency, strategic fit, and distinction between flagship and high-demand utility. | Generic feature presented as a moat, price-only differentiation, unsupported quality promise, failure to include a credible high-demand utility, missing no-go option, or recommendation that conflicts with stated constraints. |

## Reviewer Inputs

Give each reviewer the product baseline, research ledger, candidate inventory, workflow matrix, scorecard, source notes, constraints, and draft verdict. Require them to cite claim IDs and source paths/URLs for every acceptance or rejection.

The reviewers must return this compact verdict table:

| Review item | Reviewer finding | Status | Required resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| [claim/decision] | [finding] | Pass / Revise / Blocker | [exact corrective action] |

## Reconciliation Rules

1. Resolve every **Blocker** before a final verdict. Deleting an unsupported statement is a valid resolution.
2. Resolve every **Revise** item, or carry it to the final document as an explicit uncertainty that cannot influence the recommendation.
3. If reviewers disagree on the winner, the lead analyst must document the disagreement, revisit the evidence, and downgrade the conclusion to **research candidate** or **no flagship now** unless the tie is resolved by stronger evidence.
4. Do not average reviewer opinions. Evidence strength and argument quality determine resolution.
5. Do not deliver a “recommended flagship workflow” while any material evidence, quality, or constraint blocker remains open.

## Final Quality Gates

A verdict may be delivered only if all applicable gates pass.

| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| **Product truth** | The report distinguishes live verified, repository verified, documented-only, planned, and unknown capabilities. |
| **Source integrity** | Every material factual claim has a claim ID, dated source, evidence label, and stated limitation. |
| **Competitor integrity** | Paid, limited, absent, unique, first, and free claims meet the competitor-boundary protocol. |
| **Candidate completeness** | Every credible candidate is listed, including high-demand lower-value utilities; each explains what it is, who needs it, why it matters, and why it ranks where it does. |
| **Score integrity** | All eight dimensions are scored with a cited rationale and confidence; unknown evidence never earns a score advantage. |
| **Buyer choice** | A flagship has a named-alternative comparison and a non-price switching reason. |
| **Workflow quality** | The recommendation defines input, processing, decision/review, output, restore/recovery, and realistic failure/decline states. |
| **Moat realism** | The report identifies the fastest copy path and labels durability as unproven when there is no credible compounding advantage. |
| **Feasibility and economics** | Architecture, cost, privacy, integration, quality, and team constraints are checked. “Free” and “unlimited” claims are not used without credible delivery evidence. |
| **Decision integrity** | The conclusion is flagship, growth utility, research candidate, or no-go; it does not force a winner. |
| **Two-reviewer closure** | Both reviewer verdict tables are attached or summarized; no material blocker is unresolved. |

## Required Final Attestation

Place this attestation before the report references:

> **Final-quality attestation.** Reviewer A audited evidence integrity and Reviewer B challenged strategic logic. [X] blockers and [Y] revise items were identified; [state how each was resolved]. The final verdict is limited to the evidence and assumptions documented in this report. [Use “two independent review passes” instead of “two reviewers/agents” if delegation was unavailable.]

Never report “verified by two agents” unless two independent agents actually completed the reviews.
